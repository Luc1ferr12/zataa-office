Privacy Policy

Zataa Story Uploader · effective 16 October 2026 · Terms of Use

Zataa Story Uploader ("the app") is an internal tool owned and operated by Zataa (zataa.my.id). It is used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Zataa Story (@zataastory) to upload and manage that channel's own Shorts. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Use of YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services (YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API). By using the app you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Information the app accesses and stores

  • OAuth credentials (a refresh token) for the Zataa Story channel, granted by the channel owner with the scopes youtube.upload, youtube.readonly and yt-analytics.readonly.
  • Channel and video data of Zataa Story only: channel id and name, the ids, titles and descriptions of videos the app uploaded, and aggregate statistics of those videos (views, watch time, likes, comments count).

The app does not access other channels, viewers' personal data, comments' authors, or any data of people other than the channel owner.

How the information is used and shared

  • To upload Shorts that the channel owner has approved, with the title, description and visibility the owner chooses.
  • To show the channel owner a weekly performance summary of the channel's own videos.

Information is used only internally by Zataa. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with any third party.

Storage, retention and deletion

Credentials and data are stored on a private server controlled by Zataa and are not stored on any user's device. The app uses no cookies or tracking technology, and no third party serves content or ads in the app. Statistics obtained from the API are refreshed or deleted within 30 days. When access is revoked, the stored credentials and API data are deleted within 7 days.

Revoking access

The channel owner can revoke the app's access at any time through the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. Deletion can also be requested through the contact below.

Contact

Questions or complaints about privacy: Zataa · zataa.my.id · Instagram @topup.zata.
